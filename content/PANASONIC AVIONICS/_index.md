            <h3 class="fw-bold">02. Trade Studies</h3>
            <p>To determine to optimal design which balanced weight, cost, manufacturability, and pass requirements, trade studies were conducted.  The main study compared the valditiy of a monolithic CFRP vs a CFRP sandwich panel structure.  There was also extensive research conducted to determine the optimal way to mount components together to work with standard mounting hardware.  </p>

            <h3 class="fw-bold">03. Preliminary Design</h3>
            <p>Utilizing Solidwork and Siemens NX, the housing was designed using a mix of CFRP wet-layup, sheet-metal, and plastic components. The panel thickness was minimized using hand calculations with CLT analysis and refined with G2 surface modeling.</p>
