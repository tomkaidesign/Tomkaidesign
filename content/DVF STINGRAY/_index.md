            <h3 class="fw-bold">01. Setting Requirements</h3>
            <p>Based on the AIAA DBF mission profile, the aircraft was optimized to maximize score and preformance.  From this key system reqirements were defined(SR's).  The aircraft needed to support a 19.5lb payload, withstand 8G maneuvers, and keep an empty weight under 11lbs.</p>

        <div class="col-md-6 text-md-end">
            <h3 class="fw-bold">02. Trade Studies</h3>
            <p>To determine to optimal design, trade stuides were conducted comparing monocoque CFRP vs monolithic CFRP frame fuselage structures.  Using a weighted decision matrix, the monocoque design was selected for its superior strength stiffness. [Note - Initally the V1 iteration of the aircraft had a monolithic structure till the monocoque structure was proven to be structural sound]</p>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-6">

        </div>

        <div class="col-md-6">
            <h3 class="fw-bold">03. Preliminary Design</h3>
            <p>Utilizing <strong>Solidworks</strong> and a smart parametric design methodology (this fixes Solidworks breaking easy due to bad parametric modeling) the fuselage was optimized for weight, manufacturability, and serviceability.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="col-md-6">

            <p>The structure was further optimized using Classical Laminate Theory (CLT), Ansys Composite FEA and Design for Manufacturing (DFM) principles. Subcomponent testing was conducted to refine Factor of Safety (FOS).</p>
