Panasonic Avionics

The design process behind a 34in CFRP curved IFE monitor (information limited for confidentuality)

01. Defining Requirements

Based of given requirements, the monitor needed to withstand DO-160 testing, external regulations, and internal requirements. CFRP was investigated for this design because of its superior strength to weight ratio.

02. Trade Studies

To determine to optimal design which balanced weight, cost, manufacturability, and pass requirements, trade studies were conducted. The main study compared the valditiy of a monolithic CFRP vs a CFRP sandwich panel structure. There was also extensive research conducted to determine the optimal way to mount components together to work with standard mounting hardware.

03. Preliminary Design

Utilizing Solidwork and Siemens NX, the housing was designed using a mix of CFRP wet-layup, sheet-metal, and plastic components. The panel thickness was minimized using hand calculations with CLT analysis and refined with G2 surface modeling.

04. Detailed Design and DFM optimization

After performing DFM reviews with external suppliers, the geometry was optimized to mimimize cost and material used, while utilizing industry standard components when needed.

06. Final Prototype Design

The final prototype was manufactured to test the manufacturing method. Due to the timeframe of the internship of 12 weeks, there was not time to do further subcomponent testing, which would of been the next step.
