DBF STINGRAY

The process and design of the DBF Stingray Fuselage

01. Setting Requirements

Based on the AIAA DBF mission profile, the aircraft was optimized to maximize score and preformance. From this key system reqirements were defined(SR's). The aircraft needed to support a 19.5lb payload, withstand 8G maneuvers, and keep an empty weight under 11lbs.

02. Trade Studies

To determine to optimal design, trade stuides were conducted comparing monocoque CFRP vs monolithic CFRP frame fuselage structures. Using a weighted decision matrix, the monocoque design was selected for its superior strength stiffness. [Note - Initally the V1 iteration of the aircraft had a monolithic structure till the monocoque structure was proven to be structural sound]

03. Preliminary Design

Utilizing Solidworks and a smart parametric design methodology (this fixes Solidworks breaking easy due to bad parametric modeling) the fuselage was optimized for weight, manufacturability, and serviceability.

04. Detailed Design and Testing

The structure was further optimized using Classical Laminate Theory (CLT), Ansys Composite FEA and Design for Manufacturing (DFM) principles. Subcomponent testing was conducted to refine Factor of Safety (FOS).

06. Final Design

The final flight-ready fuselage met all SR's while reducing weight by 53% from the inital design.
